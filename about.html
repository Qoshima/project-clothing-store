Rooted in the steppe.
Built for the road.

Tamyr began as a question: what would nomadic outerwear look like today?

We looked to the Silk Road, to the cold plateaus of Central Asia, to the deserts of the Middle East — and to the people who crossed them for centuries. What they wore was never fashion. It was survival, passed down through generations.

Every Tamyr piece carries that lineage. The embroidery is inspired by traditional Kazakh and Bedouin motifs. The cuts are made for movement. The fabrics are chosen to last a lifetime, not a season.
