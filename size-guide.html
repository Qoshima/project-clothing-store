How to Measure

01

Chest

Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape horizontal and snug but not tight.

02

Waist

Measure around the narrowest part of your waist, usually just above the belly button.

03

Hips

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat.

04

Sleeve

Measure from the center back of your neck, over the shoulder, down to the wrist bone.
